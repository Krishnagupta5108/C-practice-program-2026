## My recommendation

Don't try to solve all 85 programs at once. Instead, solve **5–8 programs every day**. Since you're about to start engineering, focus on understanding *why* each program works rather than memorizing code.

A good daily routine is:

* **2 concept questions**
* **2 output prediction questions**
* **1 debugging question**
* **5–8 coding problems**

This approach will prepare you much better for Data Structures & Algorithms than simply rereading notes.
